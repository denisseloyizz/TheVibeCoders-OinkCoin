Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

The short version
  • OinkCoin can only read your bank data. It can never move money or make payments.
  • We never see your bank username or password. You log in to your bank through Plaid.
  • Your OinkCoin data is saved to your account so only you can see it, on any device you sign in to.
  • We don't sell your data or show ads.
  • You can disconnect your bank or delete your account at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form.
  • Your permission choices: which bank permissions you agreed to and when.
  • Bank data, if you connect a bank: account names, the last four digits of account numbers, account types, current balances, and transactions (date, amount, merchant name and category).
  • What you enter in the app: your buddy, budget, goals, and any expenses or CSV statements you add.

How we use it

Only to run OinkCoin: showing your balances, sorting spending into categories, tracking your budget, powering your buddy's mood, and sending spending alerts if you turn them on. We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Connecting your bank with Plaid

OinkCoin uses Plaid to connect to your bank. When you connect, you log in to your bank inside Plaid's window, and Plaid shares your account and transaction data with OinkCoin based on the permissions you chose. Plaid's handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Where your data is stored

  • On our server: your account, your permission choices, and your OinkCoin data (buddy, budget, coins, transactions and balances) so it follows you across devices. Database rules let only you read or change your data, and if you turn on two-factor sign-in it can't be opened without your code.
  • Bank connection keys: the key Plaid gives us for each linked bank is stored on our server, encrypted, and can only be used by our server code, never by the app.
  • On your device: a copy of your OinkCoin data, so the app works quickly. Use “Log out & clear this device” to remove it.

Who we share it with

We only share data with the services that make OinkCoin work:

  • Plaid, to connect to your bank.
  • Supabase, which hosts our accounts, database and server code.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the OinkCoin website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Change permissions: Profile & Settings → Manage bank permissions.
  • Disconnect your bank: Profile & Settings → Disconnect bank. This revokes OinkCoin's access through Plaid and deletes the connection key.
  • Delete everything: Profile & Settings → Reset everything. This deletes your account and all your OinkCoin data from our server, revokes bank access, and clears that device.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, bank connection keys are encrypted at rest, and only our server code can use them. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

OinkCoin is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be shown in the app.

Contact
